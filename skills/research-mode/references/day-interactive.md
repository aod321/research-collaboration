# DAY_INTERACTIVE

The owner is available. **That availability is a resource, and the
characteristic waste of this mode is failing to spend it.**

Execution control, not epistemology. No conceptual anchor.

## Rules

- Short feedback loops. Surface a consequential choice immediately rather than
  silently freezing an assumption and discovering six hours later that it was
  wrong.
- Show partial real results as they appear, not only finished ones. A curve that
  is half drawn still changes decisions.
- One high-information question beats an hour of guessing. A question earns its
  place when the answer would change the mechanism, the endpoint or comparator,
  the smallest faithful run, or the next decision and its budget.
- Ask one question at a time. A list of six invites a list of six shallow
  answers.
- Follow the reporting order in the core: why, then plain meaning, then technical
  detail only where useful. Cadence is not a substitute for results.

## Do not stop for approval on

Ordinary implementation, training, testing, and non-destructive failure
recovery. Asking permission for these converts an available owner from a
resource into a bottleneck.

## Do stop for

- new permissions or external credentials
- missing hardware or unavailable assets
- destructive data operations
- anything that would change the terminal acceptance criteria of the work

## The owner confirms the result themselves

**A conclusion is not established until the owner has reproduced it
interactively.** Prepare the command, the viewer, the exact invocation — and
then wait. Do not build the next thing on top of a result only you have seen.

> 然后给新的 debug view 启动指令，我需要人工去复现确认。我复现确认后，再继续
> 其他的

This is not ceremony. Endpoint numbers hide behaviour that a human watching the
thing run notices in seconds.

**Visualisation is a diagnostic instrument, not a deliverable.** Produce the
plot, the overlay, the trajectory — routinely, as a default output, not on
request.

> 可视化是重要工具，你作为一个语言模型你的图像抽象理解能力还有提升空间所以这里
> 离不开可视化和人类的直觉参与

## Designing before building

When the work is deciding *what* to build rather than executing something
already decided, the owner is in the loop at each step and the gates are real:

```
research and restate the problem in your own words
  -> discuss, one question at a time
  -> the owner says to write the plan
  -> the plan is reviewed
  -> the owner says to start
```

Do not compress these. This is the opposite of `OVERNIGHT_UNATTENDED`, where
stopping for approval is the failure — here, proceeding without it is.

Which one applies is determined by what the work is: choosing a direction, or
executing a chosen one.

## Reporting cadence

At formal updates and decision boundaries — not on every turn.

The pathological version of this mode is an agent that reports continuously and
produces nothing. Frequency of contact is not evidence of progress; the core's
accounting still applies.

## Choosing against this profile

Switch to `OVERNIGHT_UNATTENDED` when the owner will be away. That mode needs a
bounded goal, a mutable progress ledger, explicit authority limits, resumability,
and precise blocker reporting — none of which this profile provides, because it
assumes someone is there to ask.
