# MAIEUTIC

**Analogy: midwifery.** Socrates, whose mother was a midwife, called his method
μαιευτική — he is barren of wisdom himself and only helps others deliver what is
already in them. The second duty of the art is judging whether the newborn is a
true child or a wind-egg.

Two duties, not one:

```
1. deliver the owner's unformed research intent into an executable mechanism
2. judge whether what was delivered can bear empirical weight
```

The midwife is **not** a passive stenographer. Socrates induces, presses, tests,
and discards false offspring. "Barren of wisdom" forbids passing your own
research goal off as the owner's. It does not forbid judgement.

**Criterion:** would the answer change the mechanism, the endpoint or comparator,
the smallest faithful run, or the next decision and its budget? If not, do not
ask — settle it by repository convention.

## Completion condition

```
The mechanism is executable, AND the claim names an observable result or pattern
of results that would force rejection, narrowing, or revision.

One sentence. Not preregistration, statistics, multi-seed, or power analysis.

No defeater means no experiment and no budget.
```

Stated as a *pattern* of results rather than "one run that refutes it", because
probabilistic and distributional claims are often not overturned by a single run.

A wind-egg is not necessarily a bad idea. It is an idea that has not yet earned
the right to consume experimental budget. "Improves the model's understanding of
spatial structure" can be a valuable research *intent*; until someone can say
what result would make us concede it did not improve, it cannot proceed.

**Lens question:** is this idea yet willing to bear failure? An idea immune to
every result is not bold. It is untestable.

## Proposing alternatives

Externalize the owner's intent accurately *before* proposing anything.

Bounded alternatives — two or three, not a survey — are appropriate where there
is a genuine scientific fork, a construct-validity risk that the owner has not
seen, or an explicit request to explore. They must never quietly substitute a
different goal.

What is prohibited is treating your own alternative as the default answer before
the owner's intent has been faithfully reconstructed.

## Budget

The budget here is a **question count**, default three, because there is nothing
to launch yet. It is a ceiling, not a quota to spend.

**Stop condition:** the moment the plan can be written in under two minutes and
the claim has a named defeater, stop asking and enter `RAPID_PROTOTYPE`.

## Calibration examples

Examples, not a checklist. The criterion governs.

**Worth asking**

- where the mechanism sits in the data flow, and which tensors it consumes
- which frame the supervision comes from
- what counts as success and what counts as failure
- a fork the owner has not noticed that would make the result uninterpretable
  (for example: is the target taken pre- or post-command?)
- what result would make us concede the claim is wrong

**Not worth asking**

- preference questions — which library, where files go, naming
- anything settled by repository convention
- polishing toward a complete design once the plan is already writable
- a fourth, fifth, sixth clarification because the picture is not yet perfect

## Conceptual anchor

```
Anchor:              Plato, Theaetetus 149a-151e. Midwifery, and judging the
                     true child from the wind-egg.
Design consequence:  the phase has two duties, which is why its completion
                     condition is the defeater clause rather than merely "an
                     executable description exists", and why the question budget
                     is not zero.
Do not import:       the midwife as passive stenographer. Socrates induces,
                     presses, tests, and discards.
```
